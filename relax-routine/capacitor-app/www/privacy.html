Privacy Policy

App name: Relax Routine
Effective date: August 13, 2026 (this policy replaces the policy dated June 3, 2026)

1. Summary

Relax Routine has no sign-up or login and does not request any identifying information such as name, email, or phone number. Self-assessment records and settings created while using the app are stored only on your device (localStorage) and are never sent to any external server or third party. The app uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the app stores the following data in your device's browser/WebView storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.

CategoryItems (storage keys)Description
User settingsdtxrelax_user, dtxrelax_lang, dtxrelax_accent, dtxrelax_notif, dtxrelax_startDateProfile values entered in the app (e.g. nickname), language, theme color, notification settings, start date

3. Purpose of Use

  • Providing recording and review of self-assessment (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PSS) results
  • Displaying relaxation practice progress (streaks, completed sessions)
  • Keeping user settings such as language and notifications

4. Storage Location and Transmission

All data is kept solely in your device's local storage (localStorage). The app contains no external network communication code (fetch, XHR, WebSocket, etc.), and no data is transmitted or provided to the developer or any third party. For the Google Play Data safety form, this app "collects health information within the app but does not transmit it off the device."

5. Retention and Deletion

Stored data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Using the in-app reset feature, which deletes the app's stored records and settings
  • Android Settings → Apps → Relax Routine → Clear storage (Clear data)
  • Uninstalling the app, which also deletes the stored data

6. Device Transfer and Backups

If you use device backup features (e.g. Android Auto Backup) or device transfer tools, locally stored data may be included in your own backup (e.g. your Google account backup). This is a backup controlled by you through the operating system; it is not a transmission to the developer. If you do not want self-assessment records included in backups, review your device backup settings or use the in-app reset before backing up. If you share your device with others, your records may be visible to them, so we recommend using a device lock.

7. Third Parties and Advertising

This app does not display advertisements and does not use any analytics or tracking technologies. Because stored data never leaves your device, no information is shared with third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

This app creates no accounts and collects or transmits no data off the device. Accordingly, no information from any user, including children, is collected by the developer.
